About Personal Finance Dashboard

This personal finance platform was created to help you stay on top of your financial health with ease and accuracy.

Key Features:

  • Log Expenses: Quickly track daily purchases by category.
  • Bill & Subscription Tracking: Manage recurring payments, due dates, and renewal fees.
  • Visual Insights: Analyze your spending habits using interactive category pie charts.
  • Monthly Summaries: Record historical monthly performance for long-term planning.
  • Goals: Set a vacation goal or just a goal in general and keep track of progress.
  • Budgets: Set a budget and keep track.
